## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

[Churrascaria Boi Dourado](https://churrascariaboidourado.com.br/) is a **traditional steakhouse / rodízio** brand in Curitiba (since 2008). The site reads as **dark hospitality luxury**: near-black chrome (`--darker` `#0A0A0A`), **gold primary** (`--primary` `#C8A65D`), and a supporting **deep crimson** (`--secondary` `#8B0000`) for emphasis. Full-bleed hero photography of the dining room and grill sits under a fixed dark header with white logo lockup.

Archetype **polaris-friendly** (75%) with a soft **apple-glass** secondary signal: roomy section padding (`80px 0`), moderate shadows (`--shadow: 0 5px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`), and rounded menu cards (`10px`) rather than sharp brutalism. Typography splits **serif display** (Playfair Display / custom `regular`) for titles and **Ubuntu-misspelled-as-Unbuntu + Open Sans** for body — classic restaurant marketing stack, not a SaaS UI kit.

The brand identity color is unmistakably the **gold** used on “FAZER MINHA RESERVA” CTAs, badges, and active tabs — not the crimson (accent/section emphasis) and not the charcoal (structure).

**Key Characteristics:**

- Gold `#C8A65D` is the brand and primary CTA fill
- Near-black `#0A0A0A` / `#1A1A1A` frames header, hero overlays, and footer
- Crimson `#8B0000` accents H3s and deep brand moments
- Serif uppercase section titles (`h2` weight 700)
- Soft 4px buttons; 10px cards with shared `--shadow`
- Full-viewport hero with cover photography
- Portuguese hospitality UX (“FAZER MINHA RESERVA”, cardápio tabs)

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Primary

- **Boi Gold** (`#C8A65D`): `--primary` — CTA fills, badges, active tabs, brand mark companion
- **Gold Light** (`#E2D3A9`): `--primary-light` — soft highlights / hover companions
- **Gold Dark** (`#A68442`): `--primary-dark` — `.btn:hover` background

### Brand & Dark

- **Charcoal** (`#1A1A1A`): `--dark` — body text, dark bands
- **Near black** (`#0A0A0A`): `--darker` — fixed header / immersive chrome
- **Crimson** (`#8B0000`): `--secondary` — H3 emphasis, brand-red moments
- **Crimson Dark** (`#600000`): `--secondary-dark` — deeper red states

### Accent Colors

- **WhatsApp green** (`#25D366`): floating contact affordance (utility, not brand primary)

### Interactive

- Primary button: gold → `#A68442` on hover + `translateY(-2px)` lift (`0.3s ease`)
- Tabs: gold text when active (`color: var(--primary)`, weight 600)
- Cookie accept: same gold primary as CTAs

### Neutral Scale

- **Gray** (`#777777`): `--gray` — muted copy
- **Light gray** (`#E5E5E5`): `--light-gray` — borders / dividers
- **Off-white** (`#F5F5F5`): `--light` — page/card surfaces
- **White** (`#FFFFFF`): `--white` — CTA labels, hero text, logo contrast

### Surface & Borders

- Light gray surfaces for menu cards; darker translucent cards `rgba(10,10,10,0.8)` over photography
- Borders stay on `#E5E5E5`; prefer gold for interactive borders when outlining secondary buttons

### Shadow Colors

- Canonical `--shadow`: `0 5px 15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`
- Alternate: `0 4px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`

### Color Philosophy

Gold carries **hospitality prestige**; black carries **grillhouse drama**; crimson is a **secondary spice** — use sparingly so gold remains the reservation signal. Do not invent blues or teals for CTAs.

### Font Family

- **Display:** `Playfair Display` + custom `regular` / `Rough` / `urban` faces for branded headings

- **Icons:** Font Awesome 6 Brands/Free

### Principles

- Section `h2` stays **uppercase serif bold** — that is the restaurant’s editorial voice.
- Body never uses Playfair for long paragraphs — keep Unbuntu/Open Sans at 16px / 1.6.
- CTAs use **weight 600**, not black 900 — gold already carries emphasis.
- Scale hero type down: 3.5rem → 3rem → 2.5rem across breakpoints.
- Crimson on H3 is intentional hierarchy — don’t recolor every subtitle gold.
- Custom faces (`Rough`, `urban`) are ornamental; always provide Playfair/Open Sans fallbacks.

## 5. Layout Principles

### Spacing System

Practical scale from CSS: 5 · 10 · 15 · 20 · 25 · 30 · 40 · 50 · 60 · 80. Sections use **`80px 0`** vertical padding; titles often `margin-bottom: 50px`.

### Grid & Container

- Single-page marketing sections: Home · Sobre · Especialidades · Cardápio · Galeria · Localização
- Hero is full viewport minus header (`calc(100vh - 80px)`), cover background
- Cardápio as tabbed grids of image cards
- Breakpoints: **992px**, **768px**, **576px**

### Whitespace Philosophy

Very roomy vertical rhythm — churrascaria storytelling needs air between meat photography and uppercase titles. Avoid dashboard density.

### Border Radius Scale

- `4px` — buttons, badges, cookie controls
- `10px` — menu cards, modals
- `50%` — circular social/icon chips

## 6. Depth & Elevation

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Flat | `#F5F5F5` / white | Body sections |
| Ambient | Dark translucent `rgba(10,10,10,0.8)` | Overlay cards |
| Standard | `--shadow` 0 5px 15px / 20% black | Cards, header |
| Elevated | Lighter shadow 0 4px 10px / 15% | Alternate lifts |
| Deep | Full-bleed photography | Hero / galeria |
| Ring / motion | Hover lift -2px on `.btn` | Primary CTAs |

### Shadow Philosophy

Shadows are **neutral black**, not gold-tinted — gold stays in fills and type. Elevation is moderate hospitality, not material-heavy.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Do use `#C8A65D` for every primary reservation CTA
- Do set header/footer on `#0A0A0A` with white logo and links
- Do set section `h2` in uppercase Playfair-weight 700
- Do hover primary buttons to `#A68442` with a 2px lift
- Do keep menu cards at 10px radius with `--shadow`

**Don't**
- Don't make crimson `#8B0000` the primary button fill — gold is identity
- Don't replace serif section titles with Open Sans
- Don't use pure startup purple/blue accents for CTAs
- Don't drop button radius to pills (`9999px`) — brand is 4px
- Don't lighten the fixed header to white — darker `#0A0A0A` is the frame
- Don't skip the gold→dark-gold hover; static gold feels unfinished

## 8. Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|------|-------|-------------|
| sm | 576px | Stack cookie row; tighter type |
| md | 768px | Mobile menu; h1 ~2.5–3rem; hero height 500px |
| lg | 992px | Desktop nav; h1 3.5rem; full hero viewport |

### Touch Targets

Primary CTA padding `12px 25px`; mobile popup buttons go full-width with `padding: 10px`.

### Collapsing Strategy

- Hamburger `.mobile-menu-btn` replaces desktop nav under 768px
- Cardápio tabs wrap; cards stack to single column
- Cookie bar becomes column layout

### Image Behavior

Hero and galeria images are cover/crop photography — prefer art-directed JPG assets from `/img/` over abstract gradients.
